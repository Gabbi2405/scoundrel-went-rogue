## Rules of classic Scoundrel 
Scoundrel is a single-player roguelike card game played with a standard deck of cards, where you start with 20 health and try to clear the dungeon deck without dying. 
### Setup
Deck: Take a standard 52-card deck and remove all Jokers, red face cards (Jack, Queen, King of Hearts and Diamonds), and red Aces. Shuffle the remaining 44 cards to form the Dungeon deck.
- Health: Start at 20 health (maximum is 20).
- Card values: Number cards equal their face value (2–10); Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13, Ace = 14. 

### Card Types

- Clubs & Spades (Monsters): Deal damage equal to their value.
- Diamonds (Weapons): Equip to reduce monster damage.
- Hearts (Health Potions): Restore health equal to their value, up to 20. 

## Turn & Room Rules

- Entering a room: Flip four cards face-up to form a room. 
- Avoiding (running): You may skip a room by placing all four cards at the bottom of the dungeon deck. You cannot avoid two rooms in a row. 
- Facing a room: Choose and resolve three of the four cards in any order. Leave the fourth card face-up for the next room, then deal three new cards to make four again. 

### Combat & Potions

- Barehanded attack: Fight any monster without a weapon and lose health equal to the monster's full value. 
- Weapon attack: Equip a Diamond to replace any previously equipped weapon (discarding the old weapon and monsters slain with it). When attacking with a weapon, subtract the weapon's value from the monster's value; take only the remaining positive amount as damage. 
- Weapon limit: Once you defeat a monster with a weapon, that weapon can only be used against monsters with a value less than or equal to the last monster you defeated with it. 
- Potions: You can only gain health from one potion per room/turn. Extra potions in the same room have no healing effect (discard them). 

### Winning & Losing

- Lose: Your health drops to 0 or lower.
- Win: You resolve the entire dungeon deck with health remaining.
